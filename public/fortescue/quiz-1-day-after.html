QUIZ 1 (send the day after the session) - about 3 minutes

1. The gym booking agent. What did it actually do?
   a) Joined the waitlist and sent a reminder
   b) Found a flaw in the booking system and cancelled another member's spot  [correct]
   c) Emailed the gym an embarrassing rant
   d) Refused, because it would affect someone else
   Feedback: It did exactly what it took the request to mean. Before you hand an agent a task, ask what the worst literal way to meet the request would be, and add the constraints.

2. Which of these invites the AI to agree with you?
   a) "Is this a good idea?"
   b) "I think this is a good idea."  [correct]
   c) "What would a sceptical colleague push back on?"
   d) "Review this. Do not tell me whether you like it."
   Feedback: A statement gives the AI something to agree with. Ask, don't tell, then run it past a human.

3. A chatbot gives you a confident, fluent answer about something outside your expertise. What is the first question?
   a) Is this answer long enough?
   b) Which chatbot said it?
   c) Can I tell if this is wrong? If not, who can?  [correct]
   d) Should I ask it again more politely?
   Feedback: Fluent is not the same as right. Know where your expertise ends and call the colleague who could tell.

4. Which of these phrases have you heard from a chatbot?
   a) "Great question!"
   b) "You're absolutely right!"
   c) "This is a strong draft."
   d) All of the above, and probably this week  [correct]
   Feedback: Hearing the phrase is your cue. If the answer only changed because you showed doubt, start a fresh chat and ask again.

5. (Open text) Yesterday you picked one fix to try first. Which one was it, and have you had a chance to use it yet?

6. (Open text, optional) Any feedback on yesterday's session? Complaints, kudos and ideas are all welcome. Also let us know if there are particular topics you'd like to see covered in future sessions.
