Plain-text version for Microsoft Forms
QUIZ 2 (send one week after the session) - about four minutes

1. Scenario: A colleague forwards you an AI-written summary of a safety procedure and asks you to sign it off by lunch. It reads well. You have never worked on that procedure. Which question applies?
   a) If I read my instructions literally, how could they be interpreted?
   b) Am I asking a leading question?
   c) Can I tell if this is wrong? Who can?  [correct]
   Feedback: You cannot tell if it is wrong, so its fluency is doing the persuading. Find the person who owns the procedure before anyone signs.

2. Scenario: You are about to tell an AI agent: "Tidy up the shared drive and get rid of anything old." Which question applies?
   a) If I read my instructions literally, how could they be interpreted?  [correct]
   b) Am I asking a leading question?
   c) Can I tell if this is wrong? Who can?
   Feedback: "Old" and "get rid of" are wishes, not instructions. Run the pre-test prompt, then rewrite with constraints.

3. (Open text) You paste in a proposal you spent two days on and type: "I'm really happy with how this turned out. Thoughts?" Rewrite the prompt so it invites an opinion instead of agreement.
   Model answer: "Review this proposal. Do not tell me whether you like it. What would a sceptical manager push back on? Give the strongest objection first."

4. You query one figure in an AI answer. It replies "You're absolutely right!" and changes the figure. What does that tell you?
   a) The new figure is correct
   b) The first figure was correct
   c) Nothing about either figure. It changed because you pushed, not because it checked  [correct]
   d) The AI has learned from you
   Feedback: If the answer moves when you show doubt, that was sycophancy. Fresh chat, ask neutrally, check against a source or a colleague.

5. Which statement is true?
   a) A fluent, detailed answer is more likely to be correct
   b) An AI agent will stop and ask if your instruction is ambiguous
   c) Telling the AI your opinion first makes its feedback more honest
   d) None of these  [correct]
   Feedback: Fluent is not right (Sirens). Agents guess rather than ask (Monkey's Paw). Stating your view invites agreement (Narcissus).

6. (Open text) One moment this week where you used one of the three questions, or wish you had.
